I'm taking over operations at a small kayak rental business. These are the rules the previous manager wrote down. Explain how cancellation policy, inventory, schedules and pricing interact.

```
HARBOR KAYAK RENTALS - HOW BOOKINGS WORK

Schedule: three departure slots a day: 08:00, 11:00, 15:00.
  Saturdays and Sundays are "peak" days.

Inventory: 12 kayaks. Each slot can use all 12. A booking takes one
  kayak out of that slot only.

Pricing: base price is 40 for a kayak.
  Peak day: price x 1.25.
  When 3 or fewer kayaks are left in a slot: add 10 to the price.
  The price is fixed at the moment of booking.

Cancellation:
  More than 48 hours before the slot: full refund.
  Between 48 and 24 hours: 50% refund.
  Less than 24 hours: no refund.
  The refund is a share of the price the customer paid.
  A cancelled kayak goes back into that slot's inventory at once.
```
